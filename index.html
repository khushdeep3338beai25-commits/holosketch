HoloSketch AI Guide

How to Draw & Recognize Shapes

1. Hand Position: Keep one hand visible inside the camera frame, palm facing the camera.

2. Drawing: Raise only your index finger. Fold all other fingers. Move your fingertip through the air.

3. Supported 10 Shapes: Draw any closed outline — Circle, Oval, Triangle, Square, Rectangle, Diamond, Pentagon, Hexagon, Star, or Heart. Close the shape to trigger auto-snap!

4. Moving Objects: Switch to Select mode (or pinch directly over an object) to pick it up and move it.

5. 3D Lab: Use the 3D button in the toolbar to spawn interactive 3D holograms (Cube, Sphere, Pyramid, Octahedron Diamond, Cylinder).

MediaPipe vision models run completely inside your local browser GPU/WASM with zero server latency or data collection.
